What Is It?

One Device.
Endless Modes.

Mischip started as a tiny sound toy — a pocket-sized soundboard with four colorful buttons. But it grew into something much bigger. Record sounds, prank your friends, signal other drivers, or program complex automations. All from something that fits in your palm.

How It Works

Simple + Smart

1

Press It

  • Play a sound
  • Record a new one
  • Trigger an app action
  • Send a Bluetooth signal
  • Run a macro or sequence
2

Stick It

  • Built-in magnet for fridges
  • Metal tables & lockers
  • Car frames & dashboards
  • Any magnetic surface
3

Program It

  • Assign sounds, voices, or music
  • Set delays, loops, and volume
  • Create press → do X scripts
  • Sync with Bluetooth & USB-C
