§ 004 — Manifesto

Aggregated demand,
honest production.

N°1 / Cover Story

The factory is not the enemy of the small brand. The minimum order quantity is. OrderPool is a quiet cooperative for independent labels who want their goods made once, properly, by people who know how — and who are tired of bargaining alone in the shadows of an opaque sourcing economy.

“ We do not negotiate in private. The basket is filled in public; the candle is lit by everyone. ”

Each pool is a public brief. Each brief is verified. Each quote is written in the open. When the minimum is reached, escrow releases, the kiln warms, and the order ships — to a hundred buyers, in one run, at one honest price. Ten percent flat. No surcharge. No sourcing agent. No private margin built into your unit cost.

If the candle never lights, the basket empties and every cent returns. That is the whole arrangement.
